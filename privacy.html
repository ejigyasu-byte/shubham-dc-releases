Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information Sanjay Kumar Sharma collects for Shubham Debtors & Creditors and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Registration (free trial / purchase): your name, business name, email, mobile number, city, state, and optionally address and GSTIN.
  • License check: the license key, product version and a one-way code (hash) made from your computer's ID. This code cannot be used to find out details of your computer.
  • Payments: handled by Razorpay. We receive the payment status, amount and reference, not your card or bank details.

What we do NOT collect

Your accounting data (parties, bills, receipts, payments, bank statements, reports) stays on your own computer and is never sent to us. Backups are encrypted with your own Recovery Kit password, which we do not have.

How we use the information

  • To register and verify your trial and license (email OTP), activate, renew and check the license.
  • To send the license key, payment receipts and service messages about your license.
  • To provide support when you contact us.

We do not sell or rent your information. It is shared only with service providers needed for the above (payment gateway, email delivery, hosting), or when required by law.

Storage and security

License information is kept on our licensing server over secure (HTTPS) connections and only for as long as needed for the license, accounts and legal requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct your details, or to delete them when you no longer use the software (subject to legal record-keeping). Contact us at the address below.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see Contact Us.
