Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Shubham Debtors & Creditors comes with a free 45-day trial with all features, so that you can check that it suits your business before paying.

No refund after purchase

Because the full software can be tried free of cost before buying, payments for a license or renewal are not refundable once the license has been issued, and a license cannot be cancelled for a refund part-way through the year.

When we do refund

  • Double / duplicate payment for the same license.
  • Payment deducted but no license issued (for example, a technical failure) and we cannot issue the license within 2 working days.
  • An amount charged that is different from the price shown.

Write to us within 15 days of the payment with the payment reference (see Contact Us). Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5-7 working days. A refunded license is deactivated.

Cancellation

You can stop using the software at any time; the license simply ends at the end of its period and is not renewed automatically. Your data stays on your computer.
